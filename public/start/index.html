The problem

A number nobody measured is not a zero. It is a guess.

Skip a day of wearing the ring and most health apps do not go quiet. They write a zero into the gap, average it in, and tell you your recovery is down. The chart never admits the reading is missing.

You then make a real decision — train or rest, eat or don’t — on a number that was never taken. That is not a rounding error. That is the app being confidently wrong about your body.

Why now

AI can finally read your own data. It can also finally invent it.

A model can now read one person’s own record and say something specific and genuinely useful about it. That is new, and it is why JUMBO exists now rather than five years ago.

The same ability makes it effortless to say something specific, fluent and entirely made up. Every health app shipping this year has that loaded and pointed at its users.

So the hard part of building JUMBO was never getting answers out of it. It was teaching it to stop.

What makes it different

Three things JUMBO won’t do.

Everything else about this app is ordinary. These three are not, and each one is enforced in the code and covered by a test rather than promised in the copy.

– What JUMBO puts where a reading should be when you never took one. Not a zero. Not an estimate. Nothing.
  • It won’t invent a number. A gap stays a gap, on every screen, for ever.
  • It won’t pass on your name. Only a statistical summary of your data is ever sent.
  • It won’t hide its reasoning. Every insight shows its evidence and how sure it is, so you can disagree.
  • And it won’t fake a full app. Sample data is labelled everywhere, and you only ever see it if you ask.

What it does

Then five things it will.

Each one reads your own record and answers a question you actually have. None of them run off on their own: you ask, JUMBO answers.

1

Photograph a meal

Point the camera at your plate. JUMBO estimates the foods and the nutrition, shows how sure it is about each one, and you correct it before anything is saved.

2

Find the pattern

It looks across your weeks for something real — a link between how you sleep and how you train. It shows the evidence, and it will tell you when it found nothing.

3

See where it leads

A plain reading of your trajectory if the last few months carry on, and exactly which habits are driving it. Labelled as an estimate, because that is what it is.

4

Ask it anything

A straight conversation about your own numbers. It only talks about what your data actually shows, and where the data is silent it says so rather than guessing.

5

Get a session to follow

What to train today, read from your own recent load and recovery. Build the plan yourself, or have JUMBO draft one around your goal, your kit and your time.

—

And nothing it cannot do

No autonomous agents. No claims about conditions. No diagnosis. JUMBO is a wellness companion, not a clinician, and it says that on the screen rather than in the small print.

Ask JUMBO

Grounded in your own data

Every answer is built from your record, not from a general article about health. Ask it how your week went and it reads your week. Ask it something your data cannot answer and it tells you that instead of filling the silence.

Training

What to do today, and why

The suggestion comes from your own days — how many in a row you have trained, how long since the last one, where your recovery sits against your usual. It always shows the reasons, so you can look at them and decide it is wrong.

Capture

Meals, workouts, sleep, measurements, notes — all of it can be written down by hand in a few taps, and all of it counts towards your day exactly as a device reading would. JUMBO works on the web, on a phone, with nothing strapped to your wrist.
