Three things it won’t do

Everything else about Jumbo is ordinary. These three are not, and each is enforced in the code and covered by a test rather than promised in the copy.

01

Invent a number

No reading taken today? Jumbo shows a dash. It does not print zero and call your recovery poor.

02

Hide its reasoning

Every insight carries the evidence behind it and how sure it is, so you can read it and disagree.

03

Pass on your name

Only a statistical summary leaves your device. Never your name, your notes, or your photographs.
