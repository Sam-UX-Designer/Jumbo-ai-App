The problem

You are measured more than ever, and understood less.

A watch counts your steps. A ring scores your sleep. A scale logs your weight. Between them they produce hundreds of numbers a week, and not one of them tells you what any of it means for you.

So the numbers pile up, the app gets opened less, and eventually it gets deleted. Not because the data was wrong — because nobody ever read it back to you.

Why now

The thing that makes this possible is the thing that makes it dangerous.

AI can finally read one person's own record and say something specific and useful about it. That is new, and it is why JUMBO exists now rather than five years ago.

But the same ability makes it trivial to say something specific, confident and completely invented about someone's body. A wrong health number is not a wrong number. It is a decision you make about yourself.

Which is why the hard part of JUMBO was never the answers. It was teaching it to stop.

What makes it different

JUMBO will not make a number up.

Most health apps fill a gap with a zero. A heart rate nobody measured becomes “0 bpm”, and then the app tells you your recovery is poor. JUMBO does not do this, and it is enforced in the code rather than promised in the copy.

– What JUMBO shows for a reading you have not taken. Not a zero. Not a guess.
  • Nothing unmeasured is reported. A gap stays a gap, on every screen.
  • Your name never leaves the device. Only a statistical summary is sent to the model.
  • Every insight shows its evidence and how confident it is, so you can disagree with it.
  • Sample data is always labelled and never given to you unless you ask for it.

What it does

Five things, and it is honest about all five.

Each one reads your own record and answers a question you actually have. None of them run off on their own — you ask, JUMBO answers.

1

Photograph a meal

Point the camera at your plate. JUMBO estimates the foods and the nutrition, shows how sure it is about each one, and you correct it before anything is saved.

2

Find the pattern

It looks across your weeks for something real — a link between how you sleep and how you train. It shows the evidence, and it will tell you when it found nothing.

3

See where it leads

A plain reading of your trajectory if the last few months carry on, and exactly which habits are driving it. Labelled as an estimate, because that is what it is.

4

Ask it anything

A straight conversation about your own numbers. It only talks about what your data actually shows, and where the data is silent it says so rather than guessing.

5

Get a session to follow

What to train today, read from your own recent load and recovery. Build the plan yourself, or have JUMBO draft one around your goal, your kit and your time.

—

And nothing it cannot do

No autonomous agents. No claims about conditions. No diagnosis. JUMBO is a wellness companion, not a clinician, and it says that on the screen rather than in the small print.

Ask JUMBO

Grounded in your own data

Every answer is built from your record, not from a general article about health. Ask it how your week went and it reads your week. Ask it something your data cannot answer and it tells you that instead of filling the silence.

Training

What to do today, and why

The suggestion comes from your own days — how many in a row you have trained, how long since the last one, where your recovery sits against your usual. It always shows the reasons, so you can look at them and decide it is wrong.
